Solar subsidy for homes

The central government’s PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana supports rooftop solar on homes. The subsidy is paid into your bank account after your system is commissioned.

System sizeCentral subsidy
1 kW₹30,000
2 kW₹60,000
3 kW and above₹78,000 (maximum)

The subsidy is ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the third kW. Nothing extra is paid above 3 kW.

Estimated central subsidy

Rajasthan has listed an extra ₹17,000 for eligible Mukhyamantri Nishulk Bijli Yojana beneficiaries. Rules and amounts can change, so please confirm before you apply. Official site: pmsuryaghar.gov.in · Helpline 15555.

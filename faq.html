Questions and answers

How do I start?

Go to Raise a query, fill in your name, mobile number and what you want to know. We will call you back.

How is the subsidy paid?

Under PM Surya Ghar the central subsidy is paid by direct transfer into your bank account after your system is commissioned.

Does a bigger system get a bigger subsidy?

Only up to 3 kW. After that the central subsidy stays at ₹78,000, so the right size depends on your home and your bill.

What details should I keep ready?

Your latest electricity bill and a rough idea of your roof space help us give you a better answer.
